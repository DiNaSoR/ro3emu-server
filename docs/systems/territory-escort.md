# Territory escort and guild trading

This module handles the escort/cart/trade request family in `MG_TerritoryWars.proto` and `data/msgtable.tsv`. The shared territory clock supplies the absolute stage window and round. It implements 83050, 83055, 83070-83077, 83079-83082, 83084-83086, 83088, 83091-83093, 83095, 83097, 83099, 83100. It produces 31079, 83051-83054, 83078, 83083, 83087, 83089-83090, 83094, 83096, 83098 when the relevant transition occurs. Cart cargo collection uses the existing generic 32700 `NPC_INTERACTION` request, intercepted only for the escort-owned cargo UID range.

## State and conservation

`territory_escort` is one SQLite aggregate: listing escrow, guild goods balances, cart reservations, play counters and monotonic IDs. The per-role `territory_escort` column stores score and once-only claim indices. Both use `(SeasonStart, PlayBook, Cycle)` as their epoch (`TerritoryWarsClock.EpochOf`; cycle 0 keeps the `SeasonStart:PlayBook` key, and Cycle only moves with OpenContent's repeating Territory Wars): changing any of them returns unfinished listing/cart escrow once to old-epoch stock, archives that epoch, and starts or restores the new epoch without reusing its round-1 grants, trades or reward claims. Guild membership and permissions are checked against the authoritative `GuildStore` index on each mutation. A listing moves 1258000 goods from guild stock to escrow; edit replaces that reservation; takedown or end-of-trading reconciliation returns it once. An accepted application atomically moves buyer `GuildStore.Money` to seller `Money`, moves escrowed goods into the buyer's durable stock and finishes the order. It rechecks price, funds and order state inside the aggregate lock and transaction. Seller/buyer treasury updates use 32231 after commit.

**(inferred)** At each escort trading-stage entry, a guild created before the configured stage start and meeting the configured 20-member participation size receives one normal-cart load (12,000) of guild market goods 1258000. The ledger records the grant per guild and round in the same transaction as stock, so login, ticking and restart do not mint it again. This reconstructs a finite source from configured capacity: `AwardConfig.json` references 1258002 in group 1001060, while the client trade helper reads guild asset 1258000 and the supplied `PublicParamConfig` defines its trading rules. `CreditGuildGoods(guildId, amount)` also accepts a trusted territory producer credit and never accepts a client amount. Stock 1258000 is distinct from guild Materials/126 and AuctionMaterials/129. Market control uses GuildPermissionConfig right 16 (“Convoy Escort Auction”); schedule control uses 17 and advanced cart acceptance uses 18. Description length is capped at 100, listing minimum at 10 and price at configured 5-100.

Cart start atomically reserves up to the configured capacity from guild stock, requires a live escort-play stage, server-observed proximity to the configured source point, and prevents a second active cart for the same owner. A server world actor with the configured 4910001/4910002 template and NPC attribute HP follows the owner, moves with it between scenes and can be damaged by other escort guilds through CombatService. **(inferred)** The configured cart NPC is represented as a non-roaming MonsterUnit because the current world combat path targets MonsterUnit, while the cart is NPCType 2 in NPCConfig. The combat target predicate blocks the owning guild. Submit requires the same owner and guild near configured terminal 12001/12002 on map 30312, removes the reservation before success, and cannot score twice. Closing the play stage refunds carts that did not submit. The server-owned group transfer invitation records a token, recipient, scene, position and expiry.

Cart damage is processed by an awaited combat callback after effective HP change and before death. The 66%, 33%, 1% HP thresholds in `_kDamageLevelArgs` release only the incremental `_kDrops` cargo (1/1/1 normal, 3/3/3 advanced); HP, reached tier, cargo and score persist together, including a lethal crossing of all tiers. **(inferred)** Each tier removes 20% of the original goods and applies `EscortScoreParamPartRob=0.2` to that lost amount. Lethal damage consumes the remaining cart reservation and applies `EscortScoreParamRob=0.5` in the same transaction as the final damage tier, so a restart between hit and death notification cannot restore a destroyed cart. A surviving damaged cart can submit only its remaining amount. Dead or scene-loading roles cannot start, submit or collect carts, convert packs, use outpost skills or accept group transfer; stage, guild and terminal proximity are rechecked after the character lock before pack conversion. Generic 32700 with the actor UID and configured interaction 491000301 atomically marks one persisted cargo consumed and grants one 1258002 pack; the killer's guild has a 300-second pickup window, followed by free pickup until the configured 1,800-second expiry. Other generic NPC interactions continue through SceneService. `83093` consumes the player's packs and credits the guild's 126 Materials by the configured 1,000-per-pack ratio in the same character exchange transaction. **(inferred)** The configured terminal point positions from `EscortPointGroupConfig` and scene-editor data guard this request while `EscortResSubmissionNpcConfigID=200029` identifies its interaction template. `83024` is the separate territory-war drop-item request (`LC_TerritoryWarsServer.lua:772-795`), not the escort pack route (`:747-770`). Escort PvP applies configured kill/assist scores only to opposed guilds on escort maps; scene exit blocks further damage synchronously.

The five score claim indices are wire indices 0-4: `UI_Sys_TerritoryWars_PointsRward_ListWidgetCtrl.lua:231` subtracts one from its 1-based widget index. The 83088 award states are 0 locked, 1 claimable, 2 claimed (`Lua_GameDefine.lua:15749-15756`); each claim uses `AwardService` group 1001 and `CharacterService.ExchangeLockedAsync` to save item grant and role claim in one transaction. **(inferred)** All five configured thresholds route to that same group, as shown by `PublicParamConfig.EscortScoreAward`.

## Qualification boundary

`TerritoryEscortTests` uses guild bots over the game gate and a clock placed inside the configured absolute windows. It covers listing escrow, unauthorized/self/duplicate buy refusal, accepted transfer and money conservation, cart reservation and submit replay, restart/takedown idempotence, the finite once-per-round stock producer, and enemy cart damage followed by cargo pickup, replay refusal and pack conversion. Repeated proto3 fields are sent faithfully, including empty lists. NPC skill, scene-count, transfer and cargo actor presentation still need verification in the game client.
