# Skill growth implementation

`SkillService` owns AUTO_SYNC 5025 and requests 31151, 31152, 31153, 31156, 31157, 31159 and 31160. The durable `role.skill_plans` blob stores the selected zero-based index and each plan's levels, manual/auto positions, selected load levels, queue, name and genre. `RoleProgress.Skills` and `RoleProgress.SkillBar` remain the selected plan's projection for combat and quest consumers. A plan mutation saves the blob and the active projection in one SQLite transaction. `Learned` runs after an active allocation, reset or plan switch so subscribers can refresh derived effects.

The client contract is `MG_SkillGrowth.proto:36-143`. `SkillTreeConfig._iJob/_iX1..7` and the career's `_kSkillReplace` define the available groups. `SkillConfig._iMaxLevel`, `_iInitialLevel`, `_iActivateCondition` and the **desired level row's** `_kPreSkillRequired` define limits, free levels and dependencies. `JobLvConfig._iSkillPoint` grants separate rank pools. 31151 applies all target levels to a copy and validates the resulting graph and rank pools before saving, so removing a prerequisite requires the dependent refund in the same request. Zero is allowed for unlearned skills where the configured initial level is zero. 5025 includes every available group with explicit zero levels, remaining rank points and the selected plan's wheel. The normal attack uses its configured row. It fills a bar slot by default only for a Novice (the PC bar renders `whellSkills`, not `atkSkillId`), and any career can load it into a slot with 31153: the skill window lets the player drag its cell (`UI_Sys_Skill_SetItemWidget_PCCtrl` only blocks passives, unlearned skills and `PublicParamConfig` `BanSkill`/`BanSkillCanPoints`), so an Archer can put Arrow Attack on the bar.

**Normal attack by weapon.** The normal attack (5025 `currentAtkSkillId` / `skillWheel.atkSkillId`, which the client attacks with) follows `JobAttackConfig` the way the client's `Lua_CFG_JobAttackConfig.GetNormalSkillID` reads it: key = main hand `ItemConfig._iSubType` × 10 + dual wield (both hands the same subtype; the off hand's subtype when the main hand is empty, 0 bare-handed), then the job's any-weapon row (empty `_kWeaponType`), then `JobConfig._kSkillNormalID`. So a Thief with a Dagger attacks with 1005001 "Basic Attack" (ATK × 50% + 45), with two Daggers 1005201, with a Katar 1005101, and otherwise 1500001 "Normal Attack" (ATK × 50%); a Swordman with 1001001 and a Knight or Crusader with 1001101 (`JobConfig` names 1100001 / 1140001 for them). Every group a career's rows name counts as learned at level 1, and an equip, unequip or equipment plan switch resends 5025. The skill window draws the Novice tree's Normal Attack cell as this attack (`UI_Sys_Skill_AddBitWidgetBaseCtrl` swaps the Novice attack's group for `GetNormalSkillID`'s), which is why the server must report its group learned; a bar slot holding the Novice attack or another of the career's normal attacks casts the current one past Novice. Battle-mount rows (`_iMountID`) are not applied yet (**inferred** to stay on the unmounted row).

**Procs and follow-up skills** (`CombatService.Procs`). A follow-up is a server-started cast of a SkillConfig row at a target: it runs through the same effect code as a cast (damage, kill, `_kBuff2`, 31101), sends an extra-skill timeline (31121) so the client can show it beside the current action (inferred), costs nothing, starts no cooldown and rolls no further procs or extra hits (cards). A damaging follow-up always hits enemies, because some name their trigger's self-cast visual (Death Bound's 11381 selects the caster). Chance procs roll once per cast on its main target (the requested one, else the first hit; inferred), with BuffConfig `_kLogicData` read as [chance bp, row, ...]: Envenom 15111's buff (group 15120) on normal attacks, which also turns them Poison ("Basic Attack becomes Poison-property"); Fear Breeze 13303's buff [3000] on a single-target skill other than the normal attack with a bow (ItemConfig subtype 11), casting Fear Breeze 13304 of the buff's level; and Warg Strike 13312's auto-learned trigger 13317 (`_kAutoLearnSkill`) on Focused Arrow Strike 13305, at most once per the Warg Strike row's `_kDescData[6]` seconds (2.5). Envenom and Virus Spread add their own `_kLogicData` row (Poisoned 1511601-1511610) on the target. Lex Aeterna's 1216 -2000 counts in the damage (the 12xx copies of the damage stats are now their own factors, see skills-rest.md) and the buff (`_kBuffScript` Buff_Lex_Aeterna) is removed by the hit it raised. Answers to a monster's melee hit (a swing, or a skill without `_iDistanceType` 2): Parry 11210 casts its Auto Counter row at the attacker after a Physical hit, once per the skill's `_kDescData[2]` seconds; Death Bound 11311 deals the buff's `_kDescData[2]`% of the damage taken to enemies in the row's area, once per `_kDescData[3]` seconds; Auto Counter 11105 counters when its 1 s parry ends, in the row's front box, +`_kDescData[3]`% (50%) if a melee Physical hit landed during it. Venom Splasher's expiry hit is under Delayed hits below, and Warg Strike's mounted form (Warg Charge, from Wolf Taming's ride buff) under Companions. Not yet: the talent "Trigger Effect" buffs and card procs.

**Damage formulas, multi-part hits and target shapes** (`CombatService.Formulas`). `_kDamageFormulaExtra` multiplies a player's hit (inferred from the descriptions; the client only shows the terms): `T_AXadd0_etc` [1, A, attribute, 1] = 1 + A x attribute / 10000 (Blitz Beat "every 20 AGI +1%"); `T_AXadd0_etc_withlimit` the same with the attribute capped at the last value (the "Resonance 0.1%" skills: Jack Frost and the other Warlock spells DEX, Arrow Storm AGI, Axe Tornado and Earth Drive VIT, Sonic Burst and Arm Cannon LUK, Radiant Holy Light's heal DEX; the cap of 100 is read as attribute points, inferred); `AXaddBplus1(_withlimit)` [A, 115, B, limit] = (B + A x ASPD) / 10000 with the panel ASPD (attribute 115, 100 = 100%) capped at 500 (Cross Ripper Slasher "x (1 + ASPD x 0.12%)", Banishing Thrust, Rift Impact, Heavy Cannon Shot, Rolling Cutter, the Shadow skills); `T_AXadd0_Xisskilllv` is Heal's "+0.1% per Base Level", on heals only. Mode 3 tuples are flat amounts (shields) and `AXadd_HitCount_ExtraDmg` is in no description, so neither multiplies. Physical rows described as MATK-based use MATK (Magic Crasher, Double Holy Light's physical hit JudexMix1), and Soul Destroyer uses PATK + MATK. A `_kDamageParam1/2` branch is picked per target: Heal [1] on enemies, Holy Cross [1] with a two-handed spear, Soul Strike [1] on Undead (element or race, inferred), Magnus Exorcismus [1] on Undead or Demon race or a Shadow or Undead element, Shield Press/Earth Drive/Powerful and Ultra Shield Bash [1] on monsters, Sonic Blow by its target's Life (`CombatService.Passives`, skill-protocol.md). Skills that land in parts send one 31101 per part, spread from the first attack point to the last (inferred timing): Soul Expansion one per entry; Tetra Vortex Water/Fire/Earth/Wind, each rolling its own status (Frozen/Burn/Petrify/Stun) and the last `_kBuff2` tuple (Frozen 100%) on a target none reached; Gravitational Impact `_kShowDmg` [3, 1] hits of each entry; Axe Tornado's spins of [0] then [1] (the spin count from the described total: 6, or 10 Enhanced); Piercing Shadow's two slashes; Myriad Stars one 200% arrow per attack point. Single-target rows that hit more: a row with `_kRange` and `_iTargetMax` > 1 hits that area (around the locked target for release 1/7/8, target first: Virus Spread; around the caster: Evil Spirit Threat, Rolling Cutter); Blitz Beat `_kLogicData` [300, 3] adds up to 2 enemies within 3 m of the target; Sharp Shooting [3, 150] and Focused Arrow Strike (`_kDescData` 5, width 1.5 m inferred) hit a line from the caster through the target as long as the skill's range; Envenomed Blade's blade jumps up to 3 times (`_kLogicData` [4]) to a random enemy not yet hit within 3 m of the last one, 200 ms apart (inferred). Not yet: Arm Cannon's shell spread, the talent extra casts, Steal's Hiding branch.

**Class resources** (`CombatService.Resources`). `_kSkillCostParam` is only the client's meter display; the meters are EnergyConfig rows (31334) that the server fills, spends and converts from the descriptions (inferred where noted). Cross Ripper Slasher throws every blade of 540002 (starts at 4, no refill by itself): damage x max(1, blades), the meter empties, and a throw with 4 blades (`_kDescData[4]`) ends its own cooldown with the hit. Rolling Cutter adds `_kDescData[5]` = 1 blade per cast up to `[6]` = 4 (inferred: whether or not it hits). Reading Spellbook's 【Reading】 (1231001, `_kBuff1` duration -1) lasts until Stop Reading (its mode 4) or death, with no countdown; while it is on, a spellbook skill whose `_kSkillHighParam` names it (`"0:1231001:1&2:240001:N"`, or the older `"1231001:1&1233501:N"` read as pages) spends N pages of 240001 and casts its `_kAutoLearnSkill[0]` copy at the same level and target 500 ms later (the length of the "Spellbook - ..." mark it grants; inferred timing): Jack Frost, Earth Strain, Crimson Rock and Thunderclap 3 pages, Comet, Tetra Vortex and Gravitational Impact 5, Soul Expansion 1. Precision Thrust's 140001 (+1 per hit) turns 6 into one Hundred Spears charge (140002); Sonic Burst's Furious Storm (lasting) sets the enhanced charges 140003 to 5 and ends when they are spent (inferred). Shield Bash's 141002 (+1 per hit) turns 20 into 3 piles of 【Enhance Shield Bash】 1163101, which switches Shield Bash to its enhanced form; each enhanced cast spends a pile (inferred).

**Stealth and detection** use the upstream `CombatService.Stealth` and `StealthEffects` implementation described in skill-protocol.md: the client visibility mask, detection radii, reveals on damage, Ruwach, and Back Slide camouflage share one owner. The former fork callbacks are removed so a hit is counted only once and only one system publishes the visibility attributes.

**Companions** (`CombatService.Companions`). The falcon, the Demonic Wolf and the Madogear are Special Class Item leases (CareerSpecialService); the wolf and the Madogear are also battle mounts (5012 slot 2). Each skill row's `_iSkillCondition` names an `LC_ConditionList` row the server now checks before a cast (error 3018), as the client's `LC_InteractCondition` reads it: `[2001, subtype]` a worn equipment item of that subtype (2002 none), `[2003, id]` riding that battle mount (2004 not), `[2006, type]` a pendant of that PendantConfig `_iType` (1 falcon, 2 cart, 6 wolf; 2007 none); `m_eType` 1 is all clauses, 2 any. So Blitz Beat and Detecting need the falcon, the Warg skills the wolf ride, Arm Cannon and the other Madogear skills the Madogear, Dragon Breath the dragon, and spear, shield, bow, katar and dagger skills their weapon. The normal attack follows `JobAttackConfig._iMountID` like `Lua_CFG_JobAttackConfig._getData`: Warg Strike 1003201 on the wolf, Normal Punch 1631001 in the Madogear, 1100001 on the dragon, and 5025 is resent when the ride changes. A leased pendant's `_iPendantBuffID` (Falconry, Warg Accessory) lasts while it is leased, re-applied after a scene change. A ridden mount's `_kBindBuff`, Wolf's Fang and the following wolf's howl are `RidingEffects` (skill-protocol.md, Base passives); in the air a flying mount's ground bind buff gives way to its air buff. Warg Bite resets Warg Hunt's cooldown, Warg Charge and Warg Hunt reset Swift Warg's, and under King of the Wargs Warg Charge sends 4 companion hits (row 1332705, 80% each) and Warg Bite one 240% companion hit (1332805) (inferred split). Warg Hunt's melee/ranged forms and dash and Swift Warg's cast on the next step are under Forms, the next step below. Not yet: Detecting's reveal.

**Protection and detection** (`CombatService.Guard`, `BuffService.Immunity`). **Skill chains** (`CombatService.Chains`). Beta footage shows Shadow Guard's button turning into Thousand Shadows with a countdown, then into Shadow Loop, then back. **Passive skills** are upstream's: their attributes, their changes to other skills, the weapon and mount conditions and the passives that act on events (Double Attack, Double Holy Light with Holy Spirit Surge's 100%, Union, Prayer of Gratitude, Psychokinetic Astral Body, Wood Spirit's Blessing, Emergency Treatment) are described in skill-protocol.md (Talent effects, Base passives).

The live DAL's zero-based plan indexing and UI-position-minus-one conversions come from `LC_DAL_SkillDevelop.lua:1095-1136` and `LC_SkillDevelopServer.lua:115-219,244-306`. Plan count reads `PublicParamConfig.SchemeNum` (5 in the current export); the first new account receives that many unlocked plans **(inferred local entitlement policy)**. The separate `SkillSchemeNum` and `SkillSchemeCD` constants are not used for this live plan path. Switch cooldown reads generic `SchemeCD` (3 seconds in the current export) and is session-local **(inferred server policy)**. Valid unlocked plan indices are persisted, so future entitlement changes can change the saved flags without changing the wire model.

The reset request clears paid allocations in its selected plan and prunes unloaded skills without an item charge **(inferred policy)**; the client UI reset instead commits target levels through 31151. Name length reads `SchemeNameNumMax`. A nonnegative genre value is accepted; the exact original genre enumeration is unverified. Queue length reads `QueueNum`; queue load checks learned level and `SkillConfig._iQueue=1` by default. The client also checks `LC_DynamicParamMgr.GetDynamicSkillCanBeQueue` in `Lua_CFG_SkillConfig.lua:2100-2148`; `QueueEligibility` is an integration callback for the combat-owned dynamic rule. Invalid indices, unknown/unlearned groups, insufficient rank points, dependencies and combat-time bar/queue/switch/reset requests return a nonzero `ErrorCodes` value without changing durable state. The client sends `socketNum` and `queueIndex` zero-based; wheel keys are 1-based, queue keys remain zero-based. Manual and auto lists retain selected skill levels; 5025 wheel rows use those levels.

The empty queue entry `{0:SkillData{}}` and empty load-list entry are **(inferred compatibility sentinels)** for the client Lua readers of those lists. Neither creates a learned skill. On relog, the persisted plans are loaded before 5025. Legacy `skills` and `skill_bar` columns seed plan 0 once; later saves keep them as the active projection. Runtime evidence is the focused wire tests in `SkillGrowthTests.cs`; original-server entitlement, genre limits, fee, cooldown persistence and client visual behavior remain unverified.

## Card skills

A socketed card whose SpecialEffectConfig carries op Skill(3) `[3, SkillConfig row]` (`enum_SpecialEffectType.Skill`) grants that row's group at its level while it stays socketed: Heal 2004401, Disguise 2004001, Magnum Break 2004101, Envenom 2004201, Sight 2004301 and Sightless Mind 2004501, each `_iSystemType` 3 (`enum_SkillSystemType.CardSkill`). `CardEffects` sets `RoleProgress.GrantedSkills` on every scene load and socket change, and `SkillService.Level` counts it, so the bar, a load and a cast accept the skill. The skill panel lists card skills from `ServerAllSkills` (5025 `skillList`, then the selected plan's `skillAssigns` with a level above 0, `UI_Sys_SkillPanelModel.GetCardSkillList`), so 5025 sends each granted group in both. They are only sent, never saved in a plan: a plan switch checks a plan's groups against the tree. With the same group from two cards the higher level counts **(inferred)**. `CardRulesTests.ASocketedSkillCardGrantsItsCardSkill` checks the Disguise card.
